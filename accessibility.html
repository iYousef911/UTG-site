Accessibility

We want this site and the UTG app to be usable with keyboards, VoiceOver, and system settings. If something blocks you, tell us.

This website

These pages are written in HTML with a single stylesheet. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA for this marketing/legal site: text alternatives for the logo, visible focus, skip link, sufficient contrast on dark backgrounds, captions via real text (not pictures of paragraphs), and targets at least 44px on header controls.

Known limits: placeholder screenshot boxes are not real app UI; replace them with labelled images before you treat this as a finished press kit. Motion is a slow ticker; it is disabled when your system requests reduced motion.

The iOS app

UTG is SwiftUI on iOS 18+. We intend to respect system accessibility settings where the OS exposes them (VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast) as the app evolves. Watch clips depend on the source providing captions; we do not claim every clip is captioned.

Report a barrier

Email yad3v.dev@gmail.com with the page URL or app screen, your device/OS, and what you were trying to do. We will treat that as a product bug.
