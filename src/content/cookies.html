This website

There is no cookie-consent popup. We do not set advertising cookies and we do not load Google Analytics, ad pixels, or a tag manager.

Pages load a small script from our own Umami instance at https://umami.yadev.tech. It records page path, referrer, browser, OS, device type, screen size, and country. It is not used for ads. Browsers that send Do Not Track are not counted.

Your browser and the host may still create ordinary connection logs (IP address, user agent, requested path). That is separate from Umami.

We will not block Privacy, Terms, or other legal pages behind a consent modal.

The iOS app

UTG is a native app. It does not use website cookies to log you in. Optional sign-in uses Apple, Google, or Firebase. Preferences, follows, and collections may be stored on device and in iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.

Your browser controls

You can block cookies and clear site data in your browser settings. Because this site does not rely on cookies to show legal text, blocking cookies will not hide this page.
