What ships — and what is gated

Free: 3 follows · Pro+: unlimited

Upcoming releases

Track upcoming (and out-now) dates for games you follow. The free watchlist is three titles on purpose — enough for the “aha” of a personal release plan, small enough that alerts stay useful. UTG Pro+ lifts the follow limit when you want a longer list.

Pro+ for full price tools

Price tracking / Buy or Wait

Compare a current listing with historical-low context so you can wait or buy with less guesswork. Full price tools are UTG Pro+. Verdicts are informational, not financial advice. UTG is not a storefront. We do not store payment cards — Apple In-App Purchase handles Pro+ billing.

Limited free tries · then Pro+

AI game advisor + chat

Ask for a recommendation, a “what should I play,” or a second look at a title. Free users get limited tries; after that, UTG Pro+. Prompts go through our backend to OpenAI. Do not include passwords or personal data. The model can be wrong — check the catalog date before you rearrange your week.

If you send an AI chat screenshot later, it can go here as assets/ai.png.

On-device + iCloud

Collections with iCloud

Favorites and collections sync with iCloud on the Apple ID signed into the device. We do not sell your game list and we do not share it for advertising. See the Privacy Policy.

Catalogs

Free & included

Epic free promotions, Game Pass listings, and giveaways in one scan. Availability is set by those stores and by region. Confirm on the official store before you claim anything.

Local

Weather and nearby play

When-in-use location only, if you allow it: city weather and nearby game stores on the map. Not used for ads. Not required for follows, catalog, or AI.

Account

FAQ

Same questions, short

Is it free?

Download is free. Three follows, iCloud collections, catalog browse, free/included catalogs, and limited AI tries are available without paying. Pro+ is optional In-App Purchase through Apple. We do not store payment cards.

Do I need an account?

No. Sign-in is optional and skippable in onboarding.

Does it work offline?

On-device / iCloud lists can remain. Live IGDB data, prices, giveaways, AI, and notifications need a network.

How do notifications work?

Follow a game, allow alerts. We remind you about those releases. Disable: iOS Settings → Notifications → UTG.

How do I cancel Pro+?

Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel Apple billing.
