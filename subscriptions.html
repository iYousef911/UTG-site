Price

The price is shown in the App Store and on the in-app paywall at the time of purchase. It may vary by country (and by currency and tax as Apple displays them). This website does not charge you.

Payment and auto-renewal

  • Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription automatically renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period, at the then-current price shown by Apple for that product.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial (if one is offered) is forfeited when you purchase a subscription, where Apple’s rules require that.

How to cancel

Cancellation is in Apple ID settings, not on this website and not by emailing us.

iPhone / iPad: Open the Settings app → tap your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → UTG Pro+ (or the name Apple shows) → Cancel Subscription (or turn off auto-renew).

You keep Pro+ until the end of the period already paid, unless Apple states otherwise. Deleting UTG or deleting your UTG account does not cancel billing. See Delete account.

Restore purchases

Restore happens in the app, not on this site. Open UTG → paywall or Settings → Restore purchases, while signed into the same Apple ID that bought UTG Pro+.
